How to Use
  1. Select your source: URL Library (below), Manual Uploads, or Direct URLs.
  2. Pick a URL or upload file; the selection fills the current session.
  3. Adjust options on the right: batch mode, output bypass, direct URLs.
  4. Press "BallotLens" to start; watch the Debug Console for prompts.
  5. Review outputs in the Output card; download CSV/metadata if saved.
