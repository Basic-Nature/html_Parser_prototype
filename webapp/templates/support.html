OUR COMMITMENT

Transparency is part of the project, not an afterthought.

Funding must remain separate from election findings. Supporters cannot change results, source decisions, data-quality flags, or analysis. The same principle applies to our financial reporting: incomplete records remain visibly incomplete.

Financial-data publication requirements +
  • Provider payment and invoice evidence must be reconciled without double counting.
  • Founder-paid costs must be separately attributed from reimbursed expenses and any other personal tooling.

  • Unknown amounts remain unpublished rather than being displayed as zero.
  • Publication requires an approved, redacted snapshot in the private server instance directory.
03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Rules before numbers.

We will publish a financial picture only when the figures have defined sources, scopes, and a clear distinction between personal operating contributions and voluntary outside support.

01

Separate expenses from funding

A $20 operating bill does not become $20 in donations. Costs and contributions remain separate records.

02

Track funds through their lifecycle

Received, settled, spent, committed, and available amounts cannot be counted in two buckets at once.

03

Publish corrections and uncertainty

Partial months, invoice adjustments, missing data, and revisions remain visible. Unknown is never silently replaced with zero.

04

Protect independent election analysis

Financial contributions cannot influence data processing, reported results, or discrepancy findings.

Contact, refunds, and privacy

For project questions, use the contact link on the ElectionPulse Observatory. A dedicated payment-support channel and the applicable refund procedure must be verified and published before any contributions are enabled.

ElectionPulse is independently operated; it is not a registered tax-exempt charity. No tax-deductibility is claimed. Contributions cannot influence election findings, source approval, or audit outcomes.

This page does not collect or process payment-card details. Personal operator verification details must not appear in public financial snapshots. Customer-facing provider receipts and statements require separate privacy verification.
