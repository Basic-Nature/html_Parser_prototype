OUR COMMITMENT

Transparency is part of the project, not an afterthought.

Funding must remain separate from election findings. Supporters cannot change results, source decisions, data-quality flags, or analysis. The same principle applies to our financial reporting: incomplete records remain visibly incomplete.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Rules before numbers.

We will publish a financial picture only when the figures have defined sources, scopes, and a clear distinction between personal operating contributions and voluntary outside support.

01

Separate expenses from funding

A $20 operating bill does not become $20 in donations. Costs and contributions remain separate records.

02

Track funds through their lifecycle

Received, settled, spent, committed, and available amounts cannot be counted in two buckets at once.

03

Publish corrections and uncertainty

Partial months, invoice adjustments, missing data, and revisions remain visible. Unknown is never silently replaced with zero.

04

Protect independent election analysis

Financial contributions cannot influence data processing, reported results, or discrepancy findings.
